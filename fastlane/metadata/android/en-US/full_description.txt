Miras (مَرَاس) is a logistics platform for booking trucks and cargo across Saudi Arabia.

Whether you are moving home furniture, heavy equipment on a flatbed, refrigerated goods, or water tankers — inside the city or between cities — Miras gives you a fleet at the push of a button.

Customers
• Choose a service and see an instant distance-based quote in Saudi Riyal
• Confirm pickup and destination on the map
• Pay through Moyasar hosted checkout (Mada, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay). Card numbers are never typed inside the app
• Track the trip while the driver is on the way

Drivers and fleet partners
• Go on shift and receive nearby jobs
• Navigate to pickup and delivery
• Upload documents and delivery photos
• Get paid through the in-app wallet
